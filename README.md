## `01` — The identity

I’m **Abawu**, a software developer building useful products across **AI, mobile, web, and cloud**. I like working across the whole product journey—from shaping an idea and designing an experience to building the application and its delivery pipeline.

My approach is **product-first and engineering-minded**: keep the experience clear, the architecture practical, and the path to shipping repeatable. I’m especially interested in technology that solves real problems and creates opportunities in Ethiopia and beyond.
